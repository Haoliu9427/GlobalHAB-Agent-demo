02. SupplementaryData_1.zip
===========================

This compressed file contains, for each of the three key sequence sets, the original and trimmed multiple sequence alignments:
- A_Minion_LSUseqs_dataset: Kareniaceae large subunit rDNA sequences incorporating MinION amplicon sequences from this study. This tree is presented in Figure 1d.
- B_SC_PCR_dataset: Kareniaceae large subunit rDNA sequences incorporating single-cell qPCR-amplified sequences from this study. This tree is presented in Figure 1e.
- C_SA_HAB_dataset: Kareniaceae large subunit rDNA sequences incorporating sequences derived from Australian water samples prior to the 2025 bloom, including re-analysed sequences from the 2014 bloom in South Australia and in sites of New South Wales. This tree is presented in Supplementary Figure 3.


03. Figure2_Final_qPCR_data_integrated.xlsx
===========================================

This dataset contains results of qPCR-based cell abundance assessment for the distinct Karenia species across the sampling sites of this study. The columns represent:
- Site number
- Date of sampling
- Month of sampling
- Location
- Sample number
- Sampler
- Depth
- Latitude
- Longitude
- Cell abundance (cells per L) for the seven Karenia species: K. brevisulcata, K. mikimotoi, K. longicanalis, K. brevis, K. cristata, and K. hui.

These results are presented in Figure 2. 


04. Figure4c_RTgill-W1_viability_data.xlsx
==========================================

This dataset contains results of gill cells assay based on rainbow trout fish gill cells (RTgill-W1) exposed to the supernatant of stationary phase Karenia cristata SM2 for 2 h. These results are presented in Figure 4c.


05. Figure4d_4e_Invertebrate_ecotox.xlsx
========================================

This dataset contains acute ecotoxicology bioassay results for two aquatic invertebrate test organisms exposed to serial dilutions of Karenia spp. material collected from South Australia. The dataset is provided as a comma-separated values (.csv) file, with each row representing a single experimental well at the end of the assay (either 24 hrs for Rotifer- or 96 hrs for Artemia-based tests). These results are presented in Figures 4d and 4e.

The variable "Test" identifies the assay organism used in the experiment. Two assays are included in the data set: 'Rotifers', referring to Brachionus plicatilis, and 'Artemia', referring to Artemia salina nauplii (2-3 days post hatching).

"Conc" represents the concentration of Karenia cells in the exposure solution, expressed as cells per mL, based on counts of the stock sample prior to preparation of the dilution series. Concentrations were converted to cells per L for analyses and figures presented in the manuscript.

"Dead" is the number of dead organisms observed in the well at the end of the assay period. "Total" is the total number of organisms present in the well at the end of the assay (dead plus alive). Mortality for each well can therefore be calculated as Dead/Total.

"Rep" identifies the replicate dilution series (1-4). Four replicate dilution series were conducted for each assay and source combination.

"Source" identifies the origin of the Karenia material used in the assay. Two levels are present:
- 'WL' refers to freshly collected field material from West Lakes, South Australia, collected on 14 January 2026. 
- 'KC' refers to the cultured Karenia cristata SM2 line, maintained as a non-clonal culture.

Experiments consisted of serial dilution dose-response assays using either field-collected bloom material (WL) or cultured Karenia cristata (KC). Multiple concentrations were tested within each replicate dilution series. Control treatments (artificial seawater (ASW), salinity = 36, without any Karenia added) are represented by rows where Conc = 0.

Organism numbers per well varied slightly among treatments and replicates due to loading variability during assay setup. Concentrations reflect estimated exposure concentrations at the start of the assay based on counts of the stock material prior to dilution and aliquoting 3 mL into each of the four wells at the start of replicated (x 4) dilution series. Along each dilution series, 1 mL from the stock well was then removed and mixed into 2mL of artificial seawater in the next well along a dilution series (i.e. a 1/3 dilution), repeating this (1 mL into 2mL ASW) along the dilution series.
